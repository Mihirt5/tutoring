# AMC 8 author verification

The AMC 8 course was rebuilt around a 30-chapter, ~98-lesson table of contents (Combinatorics, Algebra, Number Theory, Geometry, Additional Info) supplied as a topic scaffold. Only chapter and subsection _titles_ from that source were used to plan the curriculum — no text, explanations, or problems from the source were copied or reconstructed; every lesson is authored from scratch. The prior 10-module/30-lesson structure (Number Sense, Proportional Reasoning, Algebraic Modeling, ...) has been fully retired: its lesson files, ledger entries, and publication entries were removed rather than kept alongside the new structure.

All content is newly authored. Each listed lesson contains three worked examples, two guided exercises, four independent exercises, three teaching sections, intentional hint ladders, and complete solutions.

Author self-verification is separate from the independent blind review that gates publication. The author does not change manifest publication status.

Difficulty ratings for this course now range higher than the original AMC 8 course (up to ~7–8 for optional/extension lessons such as PIE for any number of events, cubic factorizations, and advanced circle angle theorems), reflecting that several chapters in the source table of contents go beyond the actual AMC 8 test's difficulty as enrichment material — consistent with the source's own ambition and the plan approved for this rebuild.
