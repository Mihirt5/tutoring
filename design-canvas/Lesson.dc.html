Counting divisors with the exponent trick

Every positive divisor of 23·57 is built by choosing how many factors of 2 to take and how many factors of 5. Those two choices are independent — which is the whole idea.

Intuition

A divisor d = 2x·5y is legal exactly when 0 ≤ x ≤ 3 and 0 ≤ y ≤ 7. Count the pairs.

Worked step

There are 4 choices for x (namely 0,1,2,3) and 8 choices for y. By the multiplication principle the divisor count is

(3 + 1)(7 + 1) = 32
